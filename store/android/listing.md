### Full description (max 4000)

<!-- en.full -->
```
BizHub POS is the counter app for the staff of your BizHub business. Put it on a phone, a tablet or an Android POS terminal, link it to your restaurant, mini mart or guest house, and start selling. Everything your staff do shows up in BizHub for the owner, live.

ONE DEVICE, ONE COUNTER

Choose what this device does, then link it with a 6-digit code from the owner. Each staff member opens the POS with their own 4-digit PIN, so shifts, cash and who did what are always clear.

RESTAURANT

• See every table at a glance: who is sitting, for how long and how much.
• Take orders by table or for takeaway, with sizes, extras and notes.
• Send new items to the kitchen, print the bill, then take payment.
• Cash in dollars or riel, KHQR and other methods. The change is worked out for you in both currencies.
• Shifts with a cash count at the start and the end, and today's sales on the device.

MINI MART

• Sell fast: scan a barcode with the camera or a scanner, or tap a product.
• Cash, ABA and KHQR, with the change in riel.
• Print or share receipts.
• Today's sales, receipts and payments at a glance.

GUEST HOUSE

• A front desk in your pocket: rooms, arrivals, departures and money today.
• Book a room, check guests in and out, add extras and take payments.
• See every booking on the room calendar.

STAFF ATTENDANCE AND STAMP CARDS

• Attendance: a door tablet shows a QR code, or staff clock in and out on their own phone.
• Stamp card desk: scan the customer's card, add stamps and give rewards.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET

Every sale is saved on the device first. When the internet is back, it is sent to BizHub on its own, so selling never stops.

PRINTERS AND TERMINALS

• Bluetooth or Wi-Fi receipt printers, 58 or 80 mm.
• Dual-screen Android terminals show the bill and the payment QR to the customer.

MADE FOR CAMBODIA

• Full Khmer and English.
• Prices in US dollars or riel.
• Light and dark mode.

NO CODE YET?

Tap "Try a demo" on the link screen to open a sample restaurant, mini mart, guest house, team, stamp card or event and see how it works. The staff PIN is shown on the screen.

BizHub POS is free. The business owner sets up the menu, products, rooms, staff and prices in the BizHub app or at bizhubcambodia.com.

Questions? Email bizhubcambodia@gmail.com or visit https://bizhubcambodia.com.
```
